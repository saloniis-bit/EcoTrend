Why does it matter?

Fashion sustainability is not only about the fabric. It also includes how clothes are made, how long they last, how workers are treated and what happens when we no longer need them.


Think before you buy:

• Will I wear this more than 10 times?

• Do I already own something similar?

• Can I repair or reuse it?

• Is the quality worth the price?

• Can I choose second-hand instead?
